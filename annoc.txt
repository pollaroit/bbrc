Books list — what’s new on each card

Status bar (top)
• Reading or Completed: tap the status bar to open that book’s club sitting (when we have one on record).
• Admins: pencil on the right opens edit; after saving, you return to the books list instead of the book page.

Cover area
• Author names link to their author pages.
• Series shows as “#3 Series name” (number first, one line).
• Club rating when we have enough reviews; otherwise “Open Lib” instead of the longer label.
• Your own rating appears when you’ve left club feedback on that book.

Bottom stats bar
• Spreads across the full width of the card.
• Page count uses “p” (e.g. 320 p) plus publication year.
• Tap reads, reviews, shelves, or documents to go straight to that section on the book page.
• Heart shows how many members have this book in favorites (not a link).

Actions (right column)
• Same shortcuts as before — read, favorite, feedback, etc.

Card click
• Clicking the main card still opens the book; the heavy focus outline on click is gone so it feels cleaner.
